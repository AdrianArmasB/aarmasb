Adrian Armas Braithwaite

PhD Candidate in Economics

University of Michigan · Department of Economics

I am a third-year PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Michigan. My research is in macroeconomics, international finance, and trade, with a focus on emerging economies.

My current work studies the persistence of deposit dollarization and how experience-based learning shapes currency portfolio choices across generations; the macroeconomics of fertility and long-run growth; and optimal foreign-exchange intervention under segmented financial markets.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Research Analyst at the Superintendence of Banks, Insurance Companies, and Private Pension Funds in Lima, Peru. I hold M.Sc. degrees from CEMFI (Madrid) and Universidad del Pacífico (Lima).
